## How to Play

### Controls

**Keyboard Controls:**
- Arrow Keys or WASD - Move in most games
- Space - Shoot/Jump in arcade games
- Mouse - Click for UI and card games
- Number Keys - Input in Sudoku

**Game-Specific:**
- Flappy Bird: Space bar to fly
- Snake: Arrow keys to move
- Minesweeper: Left click to reveal, Right click to flag
- Sudoku: Click cell, press number
- Tic Tac Toe: Click grid position
- Connect Four: Click column to drop piece
- Hangman: Click letters
- 2048: Arrow keys to slide
- Pong: W/S keys to move paddle
- Pac-Man: Arrow keys or WASD
- Space Invaders: Arrows to move, Space to shoot
- Memory: Click cards to flip
