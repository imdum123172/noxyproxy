## 📋 Follow These 4 Simple Steps:

### **Step 1: Go to Render.com**
Visit: https://render.com

### **Step 2: Sign Up (Use GitHub)**
- Click "Sign Up"
- Click "Continue with GitHub"
- Authorize the connection
- (This is easiest method)

### **Step 3: Deploy Your Site**
1. Click **"New"** → **"Web Service"**
2. Search for: `imdum123172/noxyproxy`
3. Select it and click **"Connect"**
4. Fill in these details:
   - **Name**: `noxyproxy` (or any name you want)
   - **Environment**: `Node`
   - **Build Command**: Already filled! Just confirm
   - **Start Command**: Already filled! Just confirm
   - **Plan**: Select **"Free"** (bottom right)

5. Click **"Create Web Service"**
6. **Wait 2-5 minutes** for it to deploy...

### **Step 4: Get Your Live URL!**
When deployment finishes (you'll see a checkmark), Render will give you a URL like:
```
https://noxyproxy-xxxxx.onrender.com
```

**That's your live site!** 🎉
